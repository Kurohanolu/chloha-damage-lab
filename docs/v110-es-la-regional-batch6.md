## Evidence boundaries

Public sources are direct official ES-MX HTML from Pokémon Legends: Arceus and Pokémon Sword and Pokémon Shield, with their América Latina region control and official English counterpart identities checked. The manifest stores exact source URLs and original HTML hashes. These are **historical regional marketing names**, not claims that the older games themselves offered ES-LA localization, nor proof of Champions naming. Complete regional/form names are explicitly present, never composed by attaching a translated suffix.

Original private captures 32753, 32761 and 32763 were re-read without alteration. The bottom species strips show National Pokédex numbers, species/form names, type and weight, separate from editable party-slot names. Their translucent ability panels dim the background, but the accepted exact whole labels remain legible. The right-side move list is independent of the central ability description. That ability effect is not attributed to any move. Region remains owner-attested; language-settings screens, build and capture time are not established. No additional photographs were requested or published.
