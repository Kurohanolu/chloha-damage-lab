## Evidence chain and limitations

1. [Official Pokédex](https://www.pokemon.com/el/pokedex) search-index results returned numbered species-name rows. Six bounded queries collectively returned 1,025 distinct numbers with no name conflicts. Only the 225 exact whole names already present as unverified base-species identities are accepted. No missing rows were reconstructed from other languages or third-party data.
2. [Official es-MX press release](https://press.pokemon.com/es-MX/releases/Pokemon-Continues-30th-Celebrations-with-the-Unveiling-of-Pokemon-Wind) explicitly labels two links `Pokemon.com/LATAM`; following them yields `https://www.pokemon.com/el` and `https://www.pokemon.com/el/`. This independently identifies the regional namespace rather than relying on an assumed URL abbreviation.
3. [Official Z-A Canari page](https://legends.pokemon.com/es-mx/news/canari) names Eelektross as her partner and visibly identifies América Latina. This is an additional Z-A-specific attestation, not Champions evidence.

The Pokédex's direct page returned an anti-bot iframe. Its original HTML, current rendering and page-specific region footer were **not** retrieved. The evidence is explicitly search-index extraction, whose freshness and extraction fidelity are limited; retrieval hashes do not purport to hash original site HTML. Six original tool responses remain available for independent audit. The manifest binds their hashes, observed number/name rows and existing canonical IDs. The region link was independently followed by the evidence auditor and mandatory reviewer. No security challenge was bypassed.

Only species spellings are used. Ability/type terminology, descriptions, regional/form qualifiers, game availability and calculations are excluded. Existing English equality is an identity cross-check, not a substitute for actual Latin American source observations. All prior source records, private screenshot provenance, Expert Belt spelling/old alias, two provisional items and unsupported-content boundaries are preserved.
