Singularity is the Pokémon Damage Calculator by Chloha Labs, now at https://kurohanolu.github.io/singularity/. The migration preserves calculation behavior, nine languages, anonymous private feedback and development history. The old URL retains the audited v0.186 calculator with a localized, non-forcing route to Singularity. Owner adoption/publication approval is recorded in `docs/v0187-owner-approval.md`; actual acceptance evidence is in `docs/v0187-live-acceptance.md`. Exact-commit CI and both deployed trees are required by the release publisher.

- The user-supplied Orbital S reference is preserved unchanged. The square icon, compact header and favicon were adopted by the owner; adaptations are not pixel-identical reproductions.

- Remove the standalone `CHLOHA LABS` heading above the product name and its unused space. Keep the localized footer developer credit; use a compact, top-aligned product intro on desktop and phones. The static acceptance gate compares exact browser-reference URLs and fresh probes; a stale normal URL fails even if its cache-busting probe succeeds.
