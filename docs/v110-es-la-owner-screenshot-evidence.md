## Ambiguities resolved

- `Karatazo` is **Brick Break / かわらわり / gw0169_p022**. Existing canonical and regional records establish identity; the literal resemblance to Karate Chop is not used.
- `Ataque Centrado` is **Focus Blast / きあいだま / gw0169_s013**, not Focus Punch. The full row in 32715 corroborates the first-batch witness; the clipped row in 32713 is excluded.
- `Baya Tamar` is **Rindo / リンド / rindoBerry** and `Baya Yecana` is **Tanga / タンガ / tangaBerry**. Canonical records and separate sprite comparison reject swapping them. Supporting URLs and sprite hashes are in the manifest.
- `Golpe Pesado` is Heavy Slam. Its highlighted description in 32711 is not attached to the separate Ferroaplanadora / Steel Roller row.
- The right-hand berry detail panel in 32723/32725/32727 describes **Baya Caquic / Persim**. It does not describe the resistance berry rows on the left.
- Aggron and Mega-Aggron are fixed system footer labels in 32717, readable beneath the overlay. They are separate from the editable nickname. Both existing roster IDs are verified; no new roster entry is created.
- `Floette` and `Flor Eterna` are two fixed fields. The existing composed display `Floette (Flor Eterna)` is retained; parentheses are not claimed as captured text. `Floette Flor Eterna` is added as a joined search alias.
- `Expl. Metálica`, `At. Esp.` and `Def. Esp.` are explicitly short forms. Their evidence stays separate from full-name coverage. Metal Burst retains its prior official record; the two stat full names retain their prior evidence.
- `Firme` changes only the initial capital of the existing Adamant name; the lowercase official prose variant remains an alias. Different spellings at the same priority still fail compilation.
- Baya Anjiro is fully visible in 32727. Its clipped first-batch row remains excluded and is not retrospectively counted.
